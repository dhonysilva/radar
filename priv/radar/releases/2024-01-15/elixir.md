**Elixir** is a dynamic, functional language built on the Erlang VM. Its
actor-based concurrency model and fault-tolerant supervision trees make it
an excellent fit for services that need to stay up under load.

We've used it in production for several high-throughput services with great
results. Pairing it with `Phoenix` gives us a batteries-included web
framework without leaving the BEAM ecosystem.
